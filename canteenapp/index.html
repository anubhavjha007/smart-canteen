Order before you reach the counter

A college break is short. Standing in line to order, then standing again to wait for it to be made, eats most of it. Smart Canteen moves the ordering and waiting online, so the only queue left is the one for actually picking up food that's already ready.

  1. 1

    Order digitally

    Browse the menu and pay from your phone, from anywhere on campus.

  2. 2

    Get a token

    A unique token confirms your order and tracks its status.

  3. 3

    Pick up when ready

    Walk up to the counter only once, when your food is waiting.

What it does

🎟️

Digital token

Every order gets a unique token, so there's no confusion about whose food is whose.

📋

Live menu

Items that have run out are marked unavailable so you never order something that isn't there.

🛒

Simple cart

Add items, adjust quantities, and see your total before you pay — no surprises at checkout.

⏱️

No idle waiting

Order ahead, get on with your break, and come back only when your food is ready.
